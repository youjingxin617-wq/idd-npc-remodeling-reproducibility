## Footnote Language

Suggested shared footnote for supplementary tables:

> All single-cell comparisons were performed at the cell level and should be interpreted as exploratory. Ferroptosis scores, pseudotime values, and CellChat interactions are transcriptomic or computational inferences and do not constitute experimental validation.
